Together & Growing Every Day From My Heart

Hey you,

I wanted to write this without any grand speeches or movie dialogues, just honest words from me to you.

Looking back at when our story started on August 18, I couldn't have imagined how deeply you would become woven into my daily life. In the time since, through all our normal routines, food runs, late calls, and silly inside jokes, you have quietly become my favorite person.

First, I want to say thank you. Thank you for being in my corner, for laughing with me when things are good, and for simply being there when days feel heavy. Your patience, your warmth, and the genuine kindness you bring into my world mean far more to me than I usually manage to say out loud.

And right alongside that, I want to say from a completely sincere place: I am truly sorry. I know I haven't always been easy to deal with. There have been times when I was stubborn, when I was in a bad mood and didn't handle it well, or when I didn't listen as carefully and patiently as I should have. Whenever my words or my actions hurt you or made you feel unheard, it genuinely breaks my heart. You deserve gentleness, understanding, and care — not frustration or impatience.

"I don't expect to be perfect, but I promise you that I will never stop putting in the effort to be better, kinder, and more attentive for you every single day."

I promise to listen more, to react with patience instead of impulse, and to make sure you always know how valued and loved you are.

Thank you for sticking with me, for giving me grace when I needed it, and for choosing to walk this journey together.

Always yours,
With all my love ♥
